Cardinality Explanations
Hostel → Students (1 : N)

Each student belongs to exactly one hostel block, while a hostel houses multiple students. A mess may optionally belong to a hostel (nullable FK).

Students ↔ Messes (M : N via Subscriptions)

Resolved by junction table student_mess_subscriptions. A generated column plus UNIQUE allows only one ACTIVE subscription per student.

Attendance Swipes (1 : N)

Every swipe records student, mess, meal slot and timestamp. One swipe per student, slot and day.

Rebates & Billing (1 : N)

Approved leaves give rebate_days and a stored rebate_amount snapshot on the bill. net_payable is a generated column: base − rebate + guest + fines.

Kitchen & Waste (1 : N)

meal_optouts and approved rebates cut the headcount forecast. meal_production records kg cooked per diet line; food_waste_log records waste by stage and reason, optionally per dish (menu_id). diet_map converts dietary preference to kitchen group.
